Machine Learning Model Performance

Comparative evaluation of models for late-delivery risk prediction.

Evaluation Strategy

The models were evaluated using a temporal train-test split.

Training data: 2015–2017
Test data: 2018

This approach evaluates how the models perform when predicting orders from a later time period rather than randomly mixing historical and future records.

Accuracy

Proportion of predictions that were correct across the test dataset.

Precision

Among orders predicted as late, precision measures how many were actually late.

Recall

Among actually late orders, recall measures how many were correctly identified by the model.

Results Interpretation

The machine learning models achieved substantially higher accuracy than the majority-class baseline.

Logistic Regression achieved 69.29% accuracy, 68.62% F1-score and 75.46% ROC_AUC on the temporal test set.

Random Forest achieved 68.91% accuracy and 75.35% ROC_AUC.

XGBoost achieved 69.19% accuracy, 81.64% precision and 75.40% ROC_AUC.

These results show that the tested machine-learning approaches captured predictive patterns beyond the majority-class baseline.
